# Info for Form SB/15A (Micro Entity Certification)

**Purpose:** To qualify for 80% fee reduction.

**You must be able to certify the following:**

1.  **Small Entity Basis:** You qualify as a "Small Entity" (individual, small business < 500 employees, or non-profit).
2.  **Application Limit:** You have not been named as an inventor on more than 4 previously filed patent applications.
3.  **Income Limit:** Your "Gross Income" for the preceding calendar year did not exceed the "Maximum Qualifying Gross Income" (Approx $212,352 - subject to change, check current USPTO instructions).
4.  **Assignment:** You have not assigned or licensed the rights to an entity that exceeds the income limit.

**Filing Instructions:**
- If the online system asks for "Entity Status", select **Micro Entity**.
- You may be prompted to upload or sign a "Certification of Micro Entity Status" form.
- Use the data above to confirm your eligibility.

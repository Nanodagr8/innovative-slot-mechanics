HOW TO PLAY

GAME RULES

Select up to 10 numbers from the Hex grid. 10 numbers are drawn each round. Match numbers to win payouts based on your Risk Level.

RTP & MAX MULTIPLIERS

The game features dynamic math models. RTP and Max Win varies based on your selected risk setting:

  • Classic: 97.5% RTP (Max: 49.63x Base / 52.89x Superball)
  • Low: 97.0% RTP (Max: 782.66x Base / 687.50x Superball)
  • Medium: 96.5% RTP (Max: 882.50x Base / 658.61x Superball)
  • High: 97.0% RTP (Max: 882.20x Base / 551.09x Superball)

FEATURES

  • SUPERBALL: If the last ball drawn (10th) hits one of your numbers, your payout is multiplied by 7X! (Costs 2.5x your base bet to activate)
  • TURBO: Instant results for fast-paced gameplay.
  • NO FREE GAMES: There are no scatter symbols, free games, or bonus rounds in Hexa Keno. All payouts are awarded instantly per spin based on the paytable.

GAME MODES DESCRIPTIONS

  • Classic: The standard Keno experience providing a balanced mixture of frequent hits and reasonable maximum multipliers. (Cost: 1x Bet)
  • Low Risk: Highly frequent, smaller payouts. You are more likely to return partial bets even on few matches. (Cost: 1x Bet)
  • Medium Risk: A balanced increase in volatility, pushing peak multipliers higher while sacrificing lower-end match payouts. (Cost: 1x Bet)
  • High Risk: Extremely volatile. Zero payout on low matches, but features massive multipliers when hitting the majority of your picks. (Cost: 1x Bet)

WIN COMBINATIONS & PAYOUTS

Matches Required: To win a prize, you must match the drawn balls with your selected numbers. The number of matches required varies based on your total picks.

  • Select 1-2 numbers: Matches pay out on 1 or 2 hits.
  • Select 3-5 numbers: Matches begin paying at 2 or 3 hits.
  • Select 6-10 numbers: Matches begin paying at 3 or 4 hits depending on risk.

Dynamic Paytable: Your exact payout multipliers and cash values are continuously calculated and displayed securely in the live paytable bar located directly under the game board. Select your numbers to view real-time possible prize payouts!

Game Rules

How to Play

Select 1 to 10 numbers from the grid of 40 hexagonal tiles. 10 numbers will be drawn per round from the pool. A win occurs when the drawn numbers match your selected numbers (Win combinations range from 1 to 10 matched symbols depending on picks).

Features & Costs

  • Game Grid: 40 unique hexagon tiles numbered 1-40. All modes cost your base bet amount.
  • Superball: 10th ball drawn is the Superball. If it hits a selected number, payout is multiplied 7x. Cost: 2.5x total base bet.
  • Turbo Mode: Skips drawing animations for instant results. No additional cost.

Game Modes Available

  • Classic: Balanced hit frequency and multipliers.
  • Low Risk: More frequent partial returns.
  • Medium Risk: Higher peaks, fewer small hits.
  • High Risk: Zero payout on low matches; massive multipliers on max matches.

RTP & Possible Prize Payouts

RTP varies by mode from 96.5% to 97.5%. See individual mode for max potential wins (up to 882.50x base bet).
